your den on the internet.

A single binary you run on your own machine. It gives you a page that's yours, encrypted file transfer, and a dead man's switch — anchored to open protocols instead of anyone's platform.

curl -fsSL https://denly.xyz/install.sh | sh

Not released yet. That line is the entire install, once there's something to install.

what it is

A page that's yours

Your bio, links, and writing, at your own domain. Markdown in, plain HTML out — export the whole thing and walk away whenever you want.

Drops

Send someone a file. It's encrypted in your browser before it leaves. The key rides in the link, so the server only ever holds ciphertext.

Deadhand

A dead man's switch. Check in on whatever schedule you set. Miss enough check-ins and an encrypted payload goes to the people you chose.

Your keys

Generated on your machine, stored on your machine, never transmitted. Losing the server never means losing the keys.

the one rule everything is built around

Whoever runs the server — you, or anyone else — holds availability, not secrets. It's a constraint on the architecture, not a promise in a privacy policy.
